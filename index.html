About

I’m a third-year PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at Purdue University. My research interests center on data-driven modeling of complex systems, especially mathematical modeling of biological systems. I’m also interested in computational and applied mathematics, machine learning, and data analysis. My PhD advisor is Dr. Alexandria Volkening.

I earned my MS in Mathematics from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) in Pakistan. For my master’s thesis, I studied the dynamics of non-Newtonian fluids using computational fluid dynamics under the guidance of Dr. Meraj Mustafa Hashmi. I earned my BS in Mathematics from the University of Sargodha.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy hiking, being in nature, traveling, watching movies and TV shows, and spending time with friends and family.
